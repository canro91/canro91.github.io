When you type the last word of your book manuscript, you aren't a writer, but a marketer.

["Do good work and people will come" is a lie]({% post_url 2025-04-09-GreatWorkIsNotEnough %}).\
You have others let know about your work.\
Call it a book, app, or song.

To keep promoting my coding books, I created a new role under the Experience section of my LinkedIn profile for each book I've written.

I used _"Author of $BookName"_ as the job title, the book publication date as the starting date, and a link to the bookstore as the attached media.
