Fame isn't the same as success.

Fame is public spotlight.

Success is quiet.

It's feeling good with what you do,\
Feeling you've found a purpose,\
[Finding out what you're here for]({% post_url 2025-09-23-Reinvention %}) and doing it every day.

It's working every day,\
Putting bread in your table,\
Having a family,\
Liking what you do, getting paid for some money for it, and [feeling at ease with it]({% post_url 2025-04-08-SuccessMetrics %}).

That's a true successful person.

A lesson I heard in a Sunday sermon that made me nod in agreement and realize I'm not that far to being successful.
