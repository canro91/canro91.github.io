Nobody wants to read about the successful being successful. That’s a bad story.

James Altucher, [one of the writers I’ve stolen from]({% post_url 2026-06-27-Stealing %}), shares not to be heroes, but vulnerable. Our struggles make our stories more relatable. He has a rule: _if it doesn’t make you scared, don’t hit Publish._ I probably read it in _Choose Yourself Stories_.

In [10 Simple Ideas That Changed My Life]({% post_url 2026-03-04-SimpleIdeas %}), I wrote about [my burnout episode]({% post_url 2026-07-23-Recovery %}), [my stomach issues]({% post_url 2026-02-22-Digestion %}), and, even bits of my dating life. For a moment, I regretted sharing that, especially in a book.

These days, a reader told me the book related so much with her, because she’s been going through similar situations. That made it a 5-star for her. And that’s the power of stories.

_If it doesn’t make you scared, don’t publish it._
