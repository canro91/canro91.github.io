My days feel different when I start with email, book sales, or social media.

Daniel Miessler wrote in [Creativity vs Consumption](https://danielmiessler.com/blog/creativity-vs-consumption):

> _If there's one metric to watch for managing your happiness level, it's Creativity vs Consumption._

After [a 1-week detox]({% post_url 2026-02-24-NoSocialMediaWeek %}), I learned screens don't energize me, even when used for inspiration. 

To put limits on my consumption time:
* I set a timer for 15 minutes to read blog aggregators and news
* I have an alarm to signal the end of my working day
* On most Sundays, [I go analog]({% post_url 2026-05-18-AnalogSundays %})

Daniel's line reminds me of [Derek Sivers]({% post_url 2026-05-22-RandomIdeas %}): _stop consuming for the rest of the year and start creating_. Because [we're born to create]({% post_url 2026-07-26-Create %}). And the busiest minds are the happiest.

_Writing is my favorite way to create and the one I recommend in [10 simple ideas that changed my life]({% post_url 2026-03-04-10SimpleIdeas %})_
