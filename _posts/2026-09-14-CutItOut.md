_If it drains you, cut it out._

After two days without sleep, I almost collapsed. I had forced myself to make money online and quit my day job. For that, I started on a social media platform and challenged myself to grow it by leaving 1 post, 10 likes, and 5 comments every day.

Within days, I pressured myself to scroll faster and longer, to leave more likes and more comments. All the contradictory advice only made things worse. "Find a niche," "Don't find a niche," "Sell digital products," "Start a community," "Offer coaching calls..." Arrgggg!

But no matter the platform or your goals, doomscrolling is still draining.

Every session with a feed (or dumbscrolling, as I prefer to call it now) made my brain race and my anxiety rise. Until I couldn't sleep because my brain refused to slow down. And probably for the first time, I had a panic attack after waking up.

[Scrolling like a junkie]({% post_url 2026-02-24-NoSocialMediaWeek %}) was sucking all the energy and joy out of me with every swipe.

To reset my nervous system, I had to literally stop and [do nothing]({% post_url 2026-01-17-DoNothing %}). I didn't open any platform with a "For you" feed or with a number next to my name for a week. In the meantime, I had to go back to [walking in nature]({% post_url 2026-07-01-Walk %}), writing just for fun again, and [living away from screens]({% post_url 2026-05-18-AnalogSundays %}). I had to go back to my daily healthy rituals.

Normalize that _if it drains you, it's OK to cut it out_. Because it will cost you days to reset your nervous system and go back to your calm and steady life.

_Keeping a daily healthy ritual is one of the ideas in [10 Surprisingly Simple Ideas That Changed My Life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/). That ritual saved me from collapsing after two days without sleep._
