I don't check my analytics anymore.

My blog uses GoatCounter.\
I installed it years ago when I was playing the SEO game.\
It felt like winning a gold medal at the Olympics when my tutorials hit the first page of Google results.

But with ChatGPT and Instant Answers, [the audience of tech posts dropped]({% post_url 2026-07-13-TechContent %})—and personal blogs in general.\
Almost nobody is using search engines anymore.

And to my surprise, the other day when I opened my analytics after months, I found a spike in views.\
The funny thing was that most of traffic came from college of radiology.\
It was a bot scrapping the web to train AI.

As Dmitry Kudryavtsev wrote in [kagi.com referrer being abused by bots](https://kudmitry.com/articles/kagi-com-referrer-being-abused-by-bots/):

> _I don't mind bots scraping the web, I mean we are way past the point of being upset about that... I don't want to put stupid Cloudflare bot check or another solutions that harm the actual people who read my blog, but at this point it's practically impossible to know if anyone at all reads this blog, or any other blog on the internet._

These days, [we still write for humans]({% post_url 2026-06-06-WriteFor %})—I write for myself, to be healthy.\
But humans are not typing a search query into Google, going through a list of results, and reading every posts word by word.\
They're behind LLMs, chatbots, or agents.

We write for bots first, and incidentally for humans.
