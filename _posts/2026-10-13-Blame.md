Inspired by a post I found today on dev.to and to hit [my daily 10-idea list]({% post_url 2025-06-17-TenIdeas %}), here are 10 things every coder has blamed, or at least I have—Pair them with these [20+ signs you're a real coder]({% post_url 2025-02-19-YouAreNotAProgrammerUntil %}):

**#1. Lack of documentation:** We all complained about poor or non-existent documentation, but who wants to write it in the first place?

**#2. Unclear requirements:** _"That's what the use case/Jira ticket/user story said"_ or _"It wasn't clear from the use case..."_ That excuse never fails.

**#3. Unreadable code:** _"This code is a mess."_ Then, why do we write messy code in the first place? Or let it become messy?

**#4. Existing code:** _"That code was already there, I just tweaked it."_ Or if you're working with legacy code, written in an obscure language: _"That's from the original implementation."_

**#5. Unfamiliar codebase:** _"This is the first time I see this code..."_ Especially when you're asked to act as a rubber duck to help a coworker with a tricky bug.

**#6. QA team:** _"QA is reporting a lot of existing issues. I just came here to fix this little corner. Now I have a full page of bug reports."_ True story. It happened to a friend of a friend of mine.

**#7. A new (or old) language:** Probably this was only me when switching from desktop apps with WinForms and C# to SPAs with JavaScript back in the early 2010s. Knockout.js, anyone else?

**#8. Someone who already left the company:** _"I don't know. That's something $teammate did. He was the only one who touched that code."_ Easy to blame someone who can't defend themself. [Don't be the only one touching something]({% post_url 2025-02-18-BeingTooHelpful %}), by the way.

**#9. A tight deadline:** _"That's what the best I could think of...I only had 1 day. So..."_ Insert shrug emoji.

**#10. A more senior coder who was busy enough to help and answer my questions:** _"Oh, I asked $seniorCoder, but he was busy, and I didn't know what else to do, so I did what I could."_ Insert shrug emoji again.

And as a bonus,

**#11. StackOverflow, online tutorials, and these days, AI:** _"I don't know... that's what I found online"_ or simply _"AI did that."_

_And please don't blame [Street-Smart Coding](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/streetsmartcoding/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=10-things-every-coder-blamed) or any of my books. Jokes aside, if you want to build skills that last a career, check it out. It's the roadmap I wish I had on my journey from junior to senior coder._
