Following [my reading rules]({% post_url 2026-03-09-ReadingRules %}), I've been reading two books at a time.\
Here are the non-fiction books I've read so far:

**#1.** _[Mastery]({% post_url 2026-01-03-Mastery %}) by Robert Greene._\
Achieving mastery is like swimming:\
You can't do it against the current and you can't be your own resistance.

**#2.** _[Smart Brevity]({% post_url 2026-01-07-SmartBrevity %}) by Jim VaneHei._\
People don't read, but skim.\
Adapt to your reader's consumption habits.

**#3.** _[Glucose Revolution]({% post_url 2026-01-21-GlucoseSpikes %}) by Jessie Inchauspe._\
Control your glucose and you'll control your mood, weight, and cravings.

**#4.** _[Papyrus]({% post_url 2026-02-22-Papyrus %}) by Irene Vallejo._\
Books are the best invention of man.\
They won't disappear any time soon.

**#5.** _Financial Freedom by Grant Sabatier._\
The sooner you start, the less money you need to retire.

**#6.** _[Purpose and Profit]({% post_url 2026-04-03-PurposeAndProfit %}) by Dan Koe._\
Entrepreneurship is self-help and others-help.

**#7.** _[Anything You Want]({% post_url 2026-04-14-AnythingYouWant %}) by Derek Sivers._\
Your business is your utopia.\
You don't have to make it grow or look corporate.\
You can make it anything you want.

**#8.** _[Useful But Not True]({% post_url 2026-05-23-UsefulButNotTrue %}) by Derek Sivers._\
Your perspective is like a timezone:\
It feels "real" to you, but most people have a different one.

**#9.** _7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey._\
Rule #5: Understand, before being understood.\
Don't just listen, but listen with empathy.

**#10.** _[Emotional Intelligence]({% post_url 2026-09-20-EI %}) by Daniel Goleman._\
You can't control when emotions appear.\
But you can control how long you experience them.

Reading _Emotional Intelligence_ made me connect it to _7 Habits_.\
Which led me to adopt [reading a fortress of books]({% post_url 2026-09-09-Fortress %}).\
Building my fortress has taken me from _7 Habits_ to _Emotional Intelligence_ to _Brain Wash_.
