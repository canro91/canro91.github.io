I made a list of contributions and achievements in the past year.\
I did a quick market research. OK, by research, I mean I asked Gemini.\
I came up with a range and an anchor point.\
I reached out to an ex-coworker to ask how he asked for a raise.\
I reread the chapters on negotiation from _[Choose Yourself Guide to Wealth]({% post_url 2025-01-19-ChooseYourselfGuideToWealth %})._\
I wrote a script and had a "what would you do in my shoes?" line ready as fallback.

I texted my "boss" asking for a time slot to [discuss a raise]({% post_url 2024-11-04-RaisingRates %}).\
I was expecting to jump into a call to present my case and "negotiate."\
Instead he asked if we could do it via direct messages.

I texted something like, _"It's been a while, blah, blah, blah... My current rate is $X and I have in mind something between $Y and $Z."_

He replied, _"let me check and get back to you."_

Radio silence!

In a 1-on-1 negotiation, you get an immediate answer.\
Either "sorry, we can't do that" or "here's what I can do for you."\
But this time, a loop was opened in my mind.

The next day, about the same time, I got a text, _"Good news! Blah, blah, blah... We are supporting your request to increase your rate to $Y, effective $insertDate. Blah, blah, blah..."_

The fastest, easiest, and most awkward salary or rate negotiation I ever had.

_**Lesson:** Always go prepared. Always have a salary range and anchor point in mind. "Ask and you shall receive," Jesus said. And maybe that could be as fast and easy as some messages via Discord or Slack._
