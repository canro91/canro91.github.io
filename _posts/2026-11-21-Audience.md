Once you put your words are out there, you never know who's going to read them or how.

Yesterday, I talked to an advanced coder reading _[Street-Smart Coding](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/streetsmartcoding)_.\
To my surprise, he bought [the Spanish version](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/programacioncallejera). I don't promote the Spanish versions as much as the original ones.\
He's a native English speaker learning Spanish, and he's using my book to learn the language.\
He's even entering sentences from the book into spaced-repetition software to learn expressions and colloquialisms.\
Insert head-exploding emoji.

My analytics taught me that [mostly bots are reading my posts]({% post_url 2026-11-18-Bots %}) these days.\
And a real reader just taught me that my books work as learning material for Spanish students too.

Keep writing. You never know who's reading your words—or why and how.

_If you're a Spanish-speaking coder—or just interested in learning Spanish—you'll find my books and their Spanish versions in [my Gumroad store](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/). Look for the "Se habla español" section._
