Don't you have time to read books?

_Leave a book in your kitchen table._

This way, when you're done eating, you can steal a few minutes from your routine to pick and read a book.\
That's the perfect excuse to [reduce your scrolling]({% post_url 2026-01-22-LessScrolling %}) and pause to [ease your digestion]({% post_url 2026-02-22-Digestion %}).\
Win-win for your body, mind, and spirit.

That's the strategy I've followed to [read about 10 books so far]({% post_url 2026-09-23-Books %}).
