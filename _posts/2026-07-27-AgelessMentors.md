Nik wrote in [Make Your Mentors Ageless](https://nik.art/make-your-mentors-ageless/):

> _Make your mentors ageless, and, at minimum, you'll gain one awesome new trait: the humility to learn from anyone, no matter their relative position to you in this life._

I don't like the word "mentor."

It scares people away.
Someone you want as mentor is probably too busy for that role.
[Don't ask anyone to be your mentor]({% post_url 2024-11-26-FindingMentors %}).

I've never had official mentors, people holding the title.
But mentors are everywhere.

Reading one of [James Altucher]({% post_url 2024-11-11-LessonsFromJamesAltucher %})'s books I learn, we should believe were sent to this planet to learn from anything and everyone.

As expected most of my not-mentors and [my heroes]({% post_url 2026-04-05-FindYourHeroes %}) are older than me.

The other day, [an 80-year-old lady selling pop corn]({% post_url 2026-06-04-80YearsOld %}) taught me a lesson on work.
But I've also learned from [a 4-year-old about productivity]({% post_url 2026-05-14-MyMentorIs4 %}) and from [a 6-year old about money]({% post_url 2026-06-11-GameCards %}).

Don't be stubborn to think you have nothing to learn. Lessons and mentors are everywhere. Books are the best mentors.
