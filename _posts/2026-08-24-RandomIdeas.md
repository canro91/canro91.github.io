To clear my mind, I wrote [a 10-idea list]({% post_url 2025-06-17-TenIdeas %}) of lessons I've learned in past weeks:

**#1.** When looking for a partner, [find someone you enjoy talking to]({% post_url 2026-08-22-Dating %}).

**#2.** To meet new people, show up consistently at the same places.

**#3.** The internet has no writing or creative rules. Do what you want.

**#4.** More followers, likes, and subscriptions isn't the only goal.

**#5.** "For you" feeds drain your energy. Call it X, Instagram, Substack, LinkedIn...

**#6.** Walk to think. [Walk to change your mood]({% post_url 2026-07-01-Walk %}).

**#7.** Sometimes you have to pretend to be crazy, so you don't get crazy.

**#8.** A good night of sleep is the best productivity hack.

**#9.** Before going to bed, do a brain dump to quiet the chatter.

**#10.** Run away from you don't fit.
