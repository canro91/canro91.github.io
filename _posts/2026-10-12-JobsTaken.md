Would you use AI to change 1 line of code?

The other day, I reviewed a PR that solved a bug in a Blazor app.\
It had only 1 line of code to change a framework setting.\
Nothing from another world.\
Until I noticed the commit authors in GitHub: Claude was in there too.

I felt like _Really?!_ and closed the browser tab.
That explained the property formatted comment on top of the 1-line change.\
Comment I'd trust even less.

If we're only redirecting anything to a LLM, agent, chat, or whatever, anyone can do the same—[that's not good laziness]({% post_url 2025-07-13-TheProblemWithAI %}).\
We'll be replaced anytime soon.\
We have to be better.

_To adopt AI-assisted coding without losing your skills, follow [the C.R.U.D. framework]({% post_url 2026-10-07-CROD %})._
