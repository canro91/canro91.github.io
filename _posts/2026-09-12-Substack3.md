For this week, I had planned to leave 5 comments and 10 likes daily. But I changed my plan.

Substack "For-you" feed doesn't energize me, just like any other social platform. After a few days of engaging for growth, I noticed how my energy was depleting and my anxiety raising. [It was again a junkie]({% post_url 2026-02-24-NoSocialMediaWeek %}). I felt the pressure of growing and making money, even after a few weeks.

That's why I decided to schedule all my notes and posts, and return after 1 week. [The same plan I follow for LinkedIn]({% post_url 2026-01-19-LinkedIn %}).

Here's what happened in numbers: 

* 8 followers. Same number, but new faces.
* 3 new subscribers. Two came from Notes and the other came organically.
* 11 notes and 1 post
* 5 likes on the top performing note
* 3 likes on the next top performing one

Well, those 5 and 3 likes already feel like real progress. 

But the most important bit: I learned to grow at my own rhythm and [stop listening to everybody's advice]({% post_url 2025-02-21-StopListeningToFreeAdvice %}). And _if it drains it, it's out._
