_"Creation requires influence."_

That's a line I found in a YouTube documentary called [Everything is a remix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nJPERZDfyWc).

If creation requires influence, influence starts with copying.

As a kid, I learned to write by copying from textbooks and whiteboards.\
Then in college, I learned to code by copying code blocks from textbooks and whiteboards too.

When I decided to [take writing seriously]({% post_url 2025-07-01-SomethingSeriously %}), I learned by copying and dissecting some of my favorite writers, like [Seth Godin]({% post_url 2026-07-20-Quitting %}) and [James Altucher]({% post_url 2024-11-11-LessonsFromJamesAltucher %}).

When I read about James Altucher's bankruptcy stories, _Choose Yourself_, and his storytelling, I said _"I want to write like that."_\
He's probably the non-fiction writer I've read and copied the most.\
And by extension, I've copied the ones he copied too.

Nobody starts out original.\
For almost all [my books](/books), I can point to someone and something else where I stole ideas from.\
By "stole," I mean inspiration.

Creativity is also copying, [transforming]({% post_url 2026-11-09-Creativity %}), and combining.
