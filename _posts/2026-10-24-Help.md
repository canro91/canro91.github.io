_"Nobody becomes an icon without help."_

That was part of [Ruben Blades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rub%C3%A9n_Blades)' speech when receiving the _2026 Billboard Icon Award_.\
Sorry, I found out by doomscrolling on Instagram.\
In my defense, sometimes it gives me inspiration for ideas—like this one.

After that line, he started to mentioned a list of singers, producers, and orchestras who bet on him.

I'm not an icon, but I've received help from lots of people to get to where I'm at in my career.

One day, someone made an introduction for my first job.\
Someone gave me a chance when I had zero experience.\
Someone made another introduction when [I was fired from my first job]({% post_url 2025-01-14-BeingFired %}).\
Someone made me [sit next to him to mentor me]({% post_url 2025-10-25-LessonsFromMentor %}).\
Someone referred me to my highest-paying job.\
Someone set aside time from his work agenda to give me advice.\
Someone gave me another chance when I had a huge gap on my CV.

Those are the ones I remember from the top of my head.

I've never had the opportunity to say thanks to most of them.\
To their eyes, their help was maybe a small act of kindness without importance.\
But for me, they were defining moments in my career.

Take the time to say thank and do something small for someone today.
