_If someone recommends you a book you already read, it's time to reread it._

Last week I asked a friend for a book when you want to slow down.\
He's the kind of guy who needs a separate truck for his books when moving out.\
Of course, he had something for me.\
He told me about the author he had in mind.

When we met, he returned one book I lend him and showed me his recommendation.\
It was _Ruthless Elimination of Hurry,_ a book I already had and read.\
A perfect excuse to pick it up again.

Yes, [rereading books is fine]({% post_url 2026-04-18-Rereading %}).\
The book is the same, but we aren't.

And yes, [reading a couple of chapters is fine]({% post_url 2024-11-29-ReadingMore %}) too.\
You don't have to read from cover to cover.

He was a bit upset, but of course he had another recommendation.
