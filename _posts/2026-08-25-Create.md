_"Spending 4 hours writing a post that goes nowhere."_

That's the opening line of a rant I found on LinkedIn yesterday.

I get it!
What spend hours on something nobody sees?
The algorithm always favors trending topics and big names.

That rant reminded me of [Maria Popova's take]({% post_url 2026-06-13-TheMarginalian %}) on content.

_Don't call it content. Don't write just to fill a platform._

Instead, [write for yourself]({% post_url 2026-02-14-SomethingYouLike %}).
Write as if you were working in your garage with the door open.
You wouldn't expect everyone, but the right people to see your work.

If you choose a platform, think of every piece as a lottery ticket or practice session.
Not every lottery ticket is a winner.
Not every practice session is perfect.

Write as if nobody is reading.
Write because [you're born to create]({% post_url 2026-07-26-Create %}).

_Writing helped me recover from burnout. I've done it every day since then. And it's one of the [10 simple ideas that changed my life]({% post_url 2026-03-04-10SimpleIdeas %})._
