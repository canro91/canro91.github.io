I was looking to know how the mind and emotions work.

But I found the best lesson for marriages and relationships.

It's the **XYZ approach** from _Emotional Intelligence_ by Daniel Goleman:

When sharing about an uncomfortable or annoying situation, instead of attacking the spouse, point out the situation.
And say something like, _When you did X, it made me feel Y. I would have preferred Z._

Always make it about the situation, not the person.
And if it works for marriages, it works at the workplace too.
