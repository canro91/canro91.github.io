I don't use a todo.txt anymore.

Well, I still have one, but there aren't any new entries in there. I still keep it some done items from past years. Probably it's time to delete it.

A todo.txt or todo list is a source of stress. That's a lesson I learned from [Choose Yourself Guide to Wealth]({% post_url 2025-01-19-ChooseYourselfGuideToWealth %}).

Instead of [keeping a stressful todo list]({% post_url 2025-01-11-DitchingTodos %}), I'm starting two new lists:

**#1. Ideas.txt**. When I find an idea I'd like to try on my own work, I add it to a plain-text file with a timestamp and a source. So far, this list is full of book promotion ideas. Apart from my daily post, [writing books](/books) is what keeps me busy these days.

**#2. Curiosity.txt.** I don't want my inner child die. I want to keep asking questions and following my curiosities. That's why every time a crazy or curious question pops up, I add it to another list. A curiosity.txt. No particular format. Just one question per line.

The other day, I wonder how learning a language with a different script is. Well, that went into my curiosity list.

The next step is to pull out any of those two lists when I'm tempted to [scroll down social media feeds like a junkie]({% post_url 2026-02-24-NoSocialMediaWeek %}).
