The day before her 70th birthday, she had to rush to the ER.

She had planned the birthday party for months.
But a blood pressure spike almost ruined it.

She was discharged the same day.
But she was so anxious that she didn't enjoy her party.
Everybody noticed it, even days later.

The next week, the doctor said she only needed some pills and a diet.

Sometimes all it takes is someone reminding us that we're going to be OK.
