Here's [a 10-idea list]({% post_url 2025-06-17-TenIdeas %}) to offload my brain and empty my creative cup:

**#1. "The 5:00 club is the second most stupidest idea I've heard, other than mouth tape,"** Michael Breus, the sleep doctor, said when asked if everybody should wake up at 5:00AM. It boils down to finding your sleep chronotype and going with it.

**#2.** A consistent wake up time and getting light exposure after waking up are the easiest steps to improve your sleep.

**#3. Eight hours of sleep is a lie.** It wildly varies from 7  to 9 hours. Better to judge quality of sleep than time in bed.

**#4. _"Life is a sequence of failures punctuated by brief successes"_** by James Altucher in _The Power of No._

**#5. Pauses are a sign of love.** Count one and two before answering. Also from _The Power of No_.

**#6. If you feel stuck, it means you have a rigid routine.** Go change something about it every day. Also from _The Power of No._

**#7.** Your next non-fiction book or novel is behind 45 minutes of daily practice. From [Elizabeth Gilbert]({% post_url 2026-11-23-WritingConsistently %}).

**#8. "The Power of $Something" works like a charm as a book title.** _The Power of Now_, _The Power of When_, _The Power of No_...

**#9. Respect your readers by telling them who you're writing for.** Start your Slack messages with something like, _"If you aren't from XYZ team, you don't need to read this."_ From Hacker News.

**#10. As a writer or creative, success means aiming for more shots.** Write more. Create more. From someone on LinkedIn.

_For more not-so-random but interesting ideas, check out [10 Surprisingly Simple Ideas That Changed My Life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/)._
