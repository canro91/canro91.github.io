A book is always a good present.

But binge-watching Ryan Holiday's YouTube channel, I found the best type of books as presents:\
Daily reads.\
Books with one quote, reflection, passage, or page per day.\
Like Bible daily devotionals, _The Daily Stoic,_ or _A Calendar of Wisdom._

[Reading one page a day]({% post_url 2026-03-09-ReadingRules %}) makes your giftee remember you every day.\
Perfect idea for a crush, spouse, or love one.\
Leave a handwritten, heartful dedication on the first page...and Boom! Perfect gift.

_Although they're not daily reads, you can find [my books here](/books). They work as great presents too._
