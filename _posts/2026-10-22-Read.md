To improve my sleep, I ran an experiment before going to bed.

It was from one of [Bryan Johnson]({% post_url 2025-09-14-LivingForever %})'s videos where I learn to reduce my heart rate before sleeping.\
That's why I did for a week.

I measured my heart rate every 30 minutes:\
After some breaths to find my baseline.\
Then after some relaxing activities, like journaling, meditation, and reading.

Guess which one lowered my heart rate the most?\
[Reading a book]({% post_url 2026-03-09-ReadingRules %}).\
[I read fiction in my native language]({% post_url 2026-08-10-Translations %}), to be precise.

There's a reason why that I found when reading _[How reading books regulates your nervous system](https://bigthink.com/mind-behavior/how-reading-books-regulates-your-nervous-system/)_:

Reading involves different systems of our brain:
* Visual system to identify letters.
* Language system to give meaning to those symbols.
* Attention to keep us decoding what's in the pages.
* Memory to store what we're absorbing.

This coordination of systems moves you from "fight or fly" into "rest and digest."
Which has measurable changes in your body.

Reading regulates your nervous system.\
Another good reason to do it.
