Gabriel García Márquez shocked me in the prologue of _Strange Pilgrims._

I'm binge-reading some of his most popular books.\
[Love in Times of Cholera]({% post_url 2026-08-26-Cholera %}) was the last one.\
I'm not ready yet for _One Hundred Years of Solitude._\
But in the meantime, I picked up _Strange Pilgrims,_ a collection of short stories that take place outside his hometown.

Here's an excerpt of the prologue:

> _"When I started Chronicles of a Deaht Foretold, in 1979, I confirmed the fact that in the pauses between books I tended to lose the habit of writing, and it was becoming more and more difficult for me to begin again. That is why, between October 1980 and March 1984, I set myself the task of writing a weekly opinion column for newspapers in various countries, as a kind of discipline for keeping my arm in shape."_

In the Spanish edition, the last line reads more like _"for keeping my arm warm."_\
Maybe a reference to baseball or other sports.

Wow! A Nobel-winning writer lost his touches if he stopped writing.\
He practiced with opinion columns, something shorter, less complex than a full-length novel.

Every post is a repetition, a push-up, a practice session,\
Even if it's just a few sentences that nobody seems to be reading.\
[Better a small session than a broken habit]({% post_url 2026-01-31-BetterSmallThanNone %}).

If an athlete goes to the gym, does drills, or runs every day.\
If a Nobel-winning writer gets out of shape in the time between books.\
Every day you don't write "colds your arms."\
Write every single day.

_Writing daily helped me recover from burnout. That's one of [the simple ideas that have changed my life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/)._
