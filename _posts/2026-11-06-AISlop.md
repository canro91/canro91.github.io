AI content is too perfect... and soulless.

Em dashes were a clear giveaway of AI-generated content.\
But you can instruct your LLM of choice not to use them and still sound robotic.\
_It's. Not. That. It's. This. Beep... Beep..._

Maybe typos and imperfect sentences are a sign of human-written content.\
To show there was a human behind my words, I stopped using AI to proof-read my writing.\
You can instruct your LLM to add typos too.

Apart [from personal stories and an interesting life]({% post_url 2025-07-20-StandOutFromAI %}), brevity is maybe the true sign of a human-written content.

I stopped writing 1,000-word SEO-optimized posts long time ago.\
These days, anything longer than a tweet counts towards publishing.\
100-200 words is my sweet spot.\
AI can always add more fluff... and more fluff.

The bottleneck isn't hitting a word count, but [well-thought ideas that don't need an AI-summary]({% post_url 2025-05-05-EffectiveWriting %}).

Nobody has ever complained of good ideas being concise.
