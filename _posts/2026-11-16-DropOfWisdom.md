Since 2024, I've been writing 10-ideas a day.

To write them, [I don't use a beautiful notebook]({% post_url 2026-07-16-DirtyNotebook %}), but what I call _an ideapad_: a cheap ring notebook that fits into my back pocket.

Apart from [writing my daily ten ideas]({% post_url 2025-06-17-TenIdeas %}), I started to write proverbs, lessons, and drops of wisdom for my future self as reminders.

Here's the last one I wrote:

> _Life is what happens when when your plans don't go your way—and very rarely things ever go your way._

In recent months, [few plans turned out as expected]({% post_url 2026-10-01-LifeLessons %}).\
But they always taught a valuable lesson.
