Golden handcuffs are real.

I've had them once or twice in my career.\
The first time, it was with a challenging problem, casual Fridays, and monthly birthday celebrations.\
The next time, it was with flexible hours, decent salary, and the promise of [building any role I wanted]({% post_url 2026-09-15-BadHabit %}).

Each one had more "freedom" than the previous one.\
Just more loosen handcuffs.

Paul Glushak writes [Corporate Burnout](https://0xff.nu/corp-burnout/):

> _Today as always, men fall into two groups: slaves and free men. Whoever does not have two-thirds of his day for himself, is a slave, whatever he may be: a statesman, a businessman, an official, or a scholar._

By Paul's definition, I'm not a slave anymore.

And an aunt recently taught me a lesson:

> _Freedom is doing whatever you want, whenever you want._

By my aunt's definition, I'm almost free.

Are you completely free?
