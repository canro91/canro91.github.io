I've struggled with finding one passion and niching down.

Until I find out [it's OK to have multiple interests]({% post_url 2024-12-16-FindYourPassion %}).\
It was reassuring to learn there's nothing wrong with me, I'm just driven to multiple interests.

Recently, to avoid the niching down trap, I've switched to "umbrella" all my interests under a mission or projects.\
One of them is writing books.

A book is a perfect project to combine talents.\
Design for the cover.\
Research for the content.\
[Storytelling]({% post_url 2025-05-18-HouseMD %}) for making it compelling.\
[Copywriting]({% post_url 2025-01-16-SpainTopCopywriter %}) for the sales page.\
[Foreign languages]({% post_url 2025-05-03-LanguageHacks %}) for translations.\
Marketing for promoting it.

And you don't need to [write the next Atomic Habits]({% post_url 2025-10-07-MiniBooks %}). Start with a short book. [Here you'll find mine](/books).
