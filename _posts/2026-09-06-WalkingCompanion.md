No WiFi. No Bluetooth. No speedometer. No heart sensors. No notifications.

It only has an alarm, a countdown timer, and a stopwatch. But it beats any smartwatch. It’s water resistant and only needs a charge every 10 years. And like crock roaches, I bet I’ll survive a nuclear catastrophe. I bought a Casio AE1600H.

Because sometimes [you don’t need faster or brighter, but reliable]({% post_url 2026-04-15-Age/ %}). Sometimes you don’t need more beeps and noise, but to walk alone with your thoughts. And because corporations already know a lot about us to hand them our vitals via a a smart watch.
