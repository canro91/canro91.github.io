You don't need to win the lottery or visit an exotic place to have a perfect day.

A perfect day is rather boring and simple.

* A good night of sleep the day before.
* A moment of silence to quiet your monkey brain.
* A high-protein, nutritious breakfast.
* A workout session with upbeat music.
* A day without drama at work.
* [A good book]({% post_url 2026-08-30-GoodBook %}).
* A coffee outside with people you love.
* A walk after dinner.

You can always choose to make a day [a really good day]({% post_url 2026-09-03-GoodDay %}).

[Don't let bad weather ruin it]({% post_url 2026-08-20-BadWeather %}).

A happy life is made of happy random Tuesdays.

_Following a daily healthy practice—to make a day a perfect one—is one of the [10 Surprisingly Simple Ideas That Changed My Life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/)._
