At the peak of my burnout in 2023, I wrote a note this note on my phone:

> _"I want to wake up before the alarm rings. I want to wake up motivated and eager to work. I want to follow my morning routine: workout, writing, silence, and reading."_

At the time, finding a way out felt impossible.
It felt there was no light and no tunnel.
I thought I'd never recover.

But here I am now.
Burnout is gone.
I wake up to exercise, read, write...plus do some freelancing work.
There's still uncertainty and fear, but my mornings don't feel the same.

I was wrong! There was a way out.

Like a YouTuber recording a video for his future self, [my horizon goal is to make a living writing]({% post_url 2026-05-26-HorizonGoal %}).
