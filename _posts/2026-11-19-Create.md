Writing saved my life.

In 2024, [to recover from burnout]({% post_url 2026-07-23-Recovery %}), I challenged myself to write one blog post every day.\
I didn't know where it'd take me.\
I only knew it'd be somewhere else, a better place.

And 748 daily posts later, I was right! Writing gave me a sense of purpose.\
Coming up with 200 words made me get out of bed.\
That's [one of the ideas that changed my life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/).

[To get unstuck and reinvent your life]({% post_url 2025-09-23-Reinvention %}), like I did, create.

For me, it was writing.\
For you, it could be painting, filming, taking photos, playing an instrument, doodling...\
Anything that makes you feel alive again.\
Anything that makes you jump out of bed in excitement:
_"Oh I have another post to write today."_

James Altucher in _The Power of No_ shares an even simpler alternative:

> _Don't write a things-to-do list or a things-I-did list. Create a things-I-wish-I-did-today list. Make up stuff for that list. I wish a UFO picked me up, flew me to Andromeda, and then took me home for dinner. It's your wish list for the day that just happened. You can wish anything. These things didn't happen. The point is that you are making stuff up. You're creating._

You're already capable of creating.\
Because [you're born to create]({% post_url 2026-07-26-Create %}).\
Creativity is in your DNA.

Create something today and every day.\
Even if that's a simple things-I-wish-I-did-today list.
