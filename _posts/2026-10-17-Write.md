Write as if nobody is reading, and even if nobody is reading or engaging.

The point isn't likes, comments, or a follower count.

The point is who you become by writing.\
The discipline it gives.\
[The clear thinking]({% post_url 2024-11-08-WhyWriting %}).\
The daily practice.\
The body of work.\
[The way we look at the world to hunt for stories]({% post_url 2025-05-21-Material %}).

Write as if nobody is reading and keep writing because you don't know who's doing it.\
That's the [one of the mantras]({% post_url 2025-07-05-WritingMantras %}) that has kept me writing for years.

_Writing daily is [one of the habits that has truly changed my life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/). That's why I do it every day._
