For weeks, I've been struggling with a personal decision.

I've come up with scenarios, built a decision tree, and used AI to analyze my options. It's made me so anxious, it's often the first thing I think of when I wake up.

Yesterday, wanting a different perspective, I called my best friend to ask him what he would do in my shoes.

Among other things, he told me, _"you can't think like an engineer all the time."_ He was right! I don't want to screw this up, and I've approached it like a coder. But life is mostly about daring and taking risks.

_"Live without regrets,"_ he said near the end of our call. That's what I needed to hear because I was about to give up.
