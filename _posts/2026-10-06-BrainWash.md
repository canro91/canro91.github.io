My goal of creating a healthy and purposeful lifestyle took me to reread _Brain Wash_ by David Perlmutter and Austin Perlmutter.

Brain Wash is a science-based guide to reconnect with our brains.\
Each chapter shows the science behind healthy habits and how they affect the brain.

Being [a fan of 10-idea lists]({% post_url 2025-06-22-SimplerNoteTaking %}), here are 7 ideas I learned from it:

**#1. Disconnection syndrome.**\
Big corporations and our bad daily habits have hijacked our brains.\
We live disconnected from ourselves, from people around us, and from nature.

**#2. Your brain can heal and change.**
Between your ears, you have the most powerful device in the world.\
It's resilient and adaptable.\
You can take back the control.

**#3. What you eat affect directly your brain.**\
You're eating not for yourself, but for billions—your microbiome.
It has a directly line to your brain.\
What you eat affects how you feel.

**#4. Nature heals you and reconnects you.**\
Aim to spend at least 30 minutes in a park, at the beach, or outdoors.

**#5. Move!**\
Combine strength, cardio, and stretching.\
Exercising reduces inflammation, lowers cortisol levels, and delays Alzeihmer's.\
Inflammation is directly linked with more diseases.

**#6. What you see on a screen is designed to take you something:**\
Your data, attention, or money.\
Social media is designed to hijack your reward mechanism to keep you hooked.\
We're being robbed.

**#7. Sit in silence with your thoughts for 12 minutes every day.**\
Meditation and mindfulness literally reshapes your brain.\
Breathing deeply trains you to live calmer.
