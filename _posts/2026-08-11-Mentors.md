A friend's WhatsApp status showed a tech YouTuber giving him a warm shoutout.

It made me think of my heroes and mentors in my coding and writing career.

**#1.** I've had role models, but [I've never called them mentors]({% post_url 2024-11-26-FindingMentors %}). Someone you want as a mentor is usually too busy to accept the title.

**#2.** At one of my early jobs, I worked next to my team architects. [One of them helped me become a senior coder]({% post_url 2025-10-25-LessonsFromMentor %}). Since then, I consider him a mentor.

**#3.** If finding a mentor seems hard, books are the best mentors. [500 books equal a good mentor]({% post_url 2024-11-20-Read500Books %}).

**#4.** Even if alive, imagine your mentor is already dead. How would you learn from them? For my writing career, I've binge-watched and read James Altucher, one of my distant mentors. 

**#5.** [Make your mentors ageless]({% post_url 2026-07-27-AgelessMentors %}). Try to learn from everyone and anyone.

_If you'd like to learn from my journey, my books are a way to have me as your mentor. [Find them here](/books)._
