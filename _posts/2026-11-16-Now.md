**#1. My health:** Taking care of my health every day is my #1 infinite goal.

After burning out, I do something for my body, mind, and spirit daily.\
That's what makes me get out of bed every day these days.\
And that's part of the plan to [never suffer from burnout again]({% post_url 2026-09-01-BurnoutFree %}).

**#2. A book series:** I'm compiling 10+ years of lessons from my career as a Software Engineer into a book series.

I already wrote [Street-Smart Coding Manifesto]({% post_url 2026-07-28-StreetSmartCodingManifesto %}) and [Street-Smart Coding]({% post_url 2025-10-28-StreetSmartCoding %}).

At first, I planned a trilogy, but now I'm working on book #3 of a 4-part series.\
I've been working on it without loud announcements or launch dates.

After a recent episode of stress, I felt my writing suffered and my goal is to bring back a sense of fun and joy with this book.

**#3. Daily writing:** No matter if my impressions on social media spike or suffer, or if there's a new cool social platform, I always come back to my daily writing habit on my blog.

I began on [November 1, 2024]({% post_url 2024-11-01-SellingVsHelping %}), during a forced sabbatical after a layoff, and I have no plans of quitting anytime soon.

A daily writing habit forces me to [hunt for ideas]({% post_url 2026-11-14-NoIdeas %}), pay attention to my surroundings, and look at the world with different eyes.

_Writing daily is one of the [simple ideas that has changed my life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/)._
