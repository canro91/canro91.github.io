## Why this book?

When I started, I thought my only job was writing code.

I obsessed over syntax and lines of code.
I ignored meetings, calls, and emails.
I only wanted to code, code, and code.
But I was wrong.

After trial and error, and even getting fired, I realized:
Code alone won't save you.
It won't make you stand out either.
It's always something beyond symbols: _The skills outside the IDE._

That realization inspired my next book: **Street-Smart Coding Manifesto**.

It's for junior and intermediate coders—and my younger self—who want to create impact beyond code.

{% include image.html name="ScrollingThru.gif" width="800px" alt="Scrolling through the first pages of Street-Smart Coding Manifesto" caption="Preview of the first ~10 pages" %}

## What's inside?

It's a short guide, you can finish over coffee, to the mindset shifts and skills that create real impact.

It includes 17 lessons to help you become _a street-smart coder_:
* A coder invited to meetings for their opinion.
* A coder managers trust first for new projects.
* A coder who creates real impact beyond syntax.
