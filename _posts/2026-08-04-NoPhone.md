Yesterday, I came back home after a 3-hour road trip.

With no phone reception or WiFi, I had no other choice than to sit with my thoughts, watching the passing trees and road signs until I fell sleep. It was the longest ["do nothing" moment]({% post_url 2026-01-17-DoNothing %}) I've had in a while.

If you think [you can't leave your phone aside]({% post_url 2024-12-13-KeepingPhonesAround %}), take a road trip or a flight. Not just for the traveling part, but for the unplugging time.
