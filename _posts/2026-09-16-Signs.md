I spent three days on medication before I could leave the bathroom.

Everything I ate sent me to the toilet. I even had to call a doctor to get a prescription over the phone, just to be able to get out of the house for an appropriate doctor's appointment. It was [the peak of my burnout]({% post_url 2026-08-23-Affirmation %}), in 2023. 

It took me days before I could [eat without fear]({% post_url 2026-02-22-Digestion %}). My job was such a pain that I wanted to finish my daily tasks as soon as I could, and I skipped meals and had irregular eating schedules. Bad idea!

Getting stomach sick was a loud siren, but there was a more subtle sign I didn't notice.

For weeks, I didn't want to get out of bed. I thought there was no point! Every day felt and looked the same. Another pointless meeting. Another boring task. Another mark on the wall. I waited until the last minute to rush from my bed to my meetings. Thank goodness they were over _Teams_, and nobody noticed I didn't shower or my breath.

That apathy and disengagement started Sunday evenings. Just imagining what the next Monday would look like already gave me a funny feeling in my chest and stomach. It wasn't love butterflies.

I tried taking vacations and sick days. But once I was back, my energy got depleted after a few days. Back to square one, trapped on my bed again.

Some days you're just tired and don't want to get up. But if that's multiple days in a row, don't wait to fall sick. That's the first sign to look for.
