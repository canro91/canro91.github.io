Read broadly or read about certain subjects or reread the same books?

I've struggled choosing [a reading strategy]({% post_url 2025-01-09-ReadingStrategy %}).
Probably, for years, I read about anything that crossed my path.
But I realized I was only [reading to chase quantity]({% post_url 2024-11-29-ReadingMore %}).
Then I switched to read books as needed.

These days, I started _Emotional Intelligence_ by Daniel Goleman.
It teaches about _empathic listening_: not just hearing or repeating the last words you hear, but to listen and paraphrase showing the underlying emotion.

While reading about it, I remember that's precisely one of the habits from _7 Habits of Highly Effective People._
That made me go back and read that chapter again.
Habit #5, if I remember correctly.

Which led me to remember about Anthony Metivier's idea of building a fortress of books.
In of his YouTube videos, he recommends [reading books]({% post_url 2026-03-09-ReadingRules %}) that build on, expand, or contradict each other.
You'll find the same concepts in different perspectives.
That's a better reading strategy.
