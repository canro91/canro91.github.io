Yesterday, [Street-Smart Coding, my first book, turned one year]({% post_url 2026-10-28-OneYear %}).

It isn't a New York Times best-seller... yet?\
It doesn't pay my rent... yet?\
It's just another tiny piece of content filling the Internet ether.\
But it's a big small win for me.

That's why I broke my weekly routine and went out to eat a slice of pizza or two.\
To make it more symbolic, I paid for it with the book sales.\
By the way, I ate some salad and protein before, to [control the glucose spikes]({% post_url 2026-01-21-GlucoseSpikes %}).\
Even I took some pictures to remember it.

Some say, happiness is about the journey, not the destination.\
Just make sure to celebrate those small moments along it.

I'm [doing nothing]({% post_url 2026-01-17-DoNothing %}) the rest of the day off to keep celebrating.
