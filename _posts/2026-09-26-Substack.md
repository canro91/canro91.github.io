I challenged myself to write 30 notes and 4 posts in Substack, but it cost me my sanity.

I chose Substack after being shadow-banned from Medium and, I have to admit it, I wanted some attention and book sales. But...

In past weeks, I've gone throught an episode of stress and anxiety. My head has been rushing with ruminating thoughts and [time traveling]({% post_url 2024-12-19-TimeTravel %}), and trying to hit a quota of likes and comments to get subscribers and followers only added more oxygen and fuel to the fire. I was so anxious I woke up in the middle of the night and couldn't get back to sleep. I needed to work hard to [get back to my steady lifestyle]({% post_url 2026-09-14-CutItOut %}).

I quit engaging at all, and scheduled all my notes and posts instead.

## Some random numbers

In spite of my lack of engagement in the last two weeks, here are some random numbers from my experiment:

* 8 followers and 7 subscribers.
* 45 notes and 4 posts written.
* 6 likes on my top-2 performing notes.
* 5 views, 1 like, and 1 comment on my top performing post.

One. Comment. On. A. Post. OMG! That already felt like going viral.

Here's [my #1 note](https://substack.com/@iamcesaraguirre/note/c-326016587):

> One small daily choice that saved me from burnout:
Do something for my body, mind, and spirit every day.
True health means the three in balance.
Work on each one every day. No excuses!
That’s a simple recipe for a happy life.

And here's my #1 post:

_[10 Reasons Why I Write 10 Bad Ideas](https://iamcesaraguirre.substack.com/p/10-reasons-why-i-write-10-bad-ideas)_

Yes, I tweaked a post I already shared here on my blog. Don't call the Internet police, please.

## Some lessons

Subscriber and follower count meant almost nothing, but here are the most important bits from my experiment:

**#1.** No matter the application, doomscrolling is always doomscrolling... and everything shown in a screen is most likely designed to steal your attention.

**#2.** You don't have to grow or make money. Don't listen to gurus selling you growth tactics. It's OK to grow at your own pace... or to quit.

**#3.** Good ideas stand on their own, without hacks.

**#4.** If it drains you, cut it out. Your sanity is more important than a Monopoly money next to your name on a platform.

**#5.** Time in front of a screen is time away from yourself and from real interaction.

I'm keeping my Substack account to distribute my long-form posts to a wider audience, but [strong, mindful participation rules]({% post_url 2026-01-19-LinkedIn %}): Only for 1 hour a week to schedule my content. Nothing more.
