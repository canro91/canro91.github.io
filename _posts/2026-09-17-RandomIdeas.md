To keep my creative muscles in shape, I write [10 bad ideas every day]({% post_url 2025-06-17-TenIdeas %}). Here's today's list:

**#1.** Stress weakens your immune system. A lesson from _Emotional Intelligence_ by Daniel Goleman.

**#2.** [Use the XYZ approach]({% post_url 2026-09-10-Marriages %}) when discussing with your partner or at work.

**#3.** Reading regulates your nervous system.

**#4.** To remember what you read, ask _"What am I trying to achieve?"_

**#5.** _Tsundoku_ is the Japanese word for a pile of unread books.

**#6.** In a book date, you take your date to a bookstore, discuss about the books you both have read, then each one buys a book the other person will like.

**#7.** [Ban the word Discover from your copy]({% post_url 2026-09-07-BannedWords %}).

**#8.** Burnout isn't the same as boreout. The first is from overwork and the last one from underwork. Technically, [I didn't burned out, but bored out]({% post_url 2026-09-16-Signs %}).

**#9.** ["Your perfect life won't be perfect."]({% post_url 2026-09-08-PerfectLife %})

**#10.** _"When you learn, you earn."_ A line from [Skip The Line]({% post_url 2025-05-27-SkipTheLine %}) I missed the first time I read it.
