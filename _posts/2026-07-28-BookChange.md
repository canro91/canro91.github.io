I don't want to write the same book twice.

I challenge myself to make every book different.
So far, I've experimented with formats: a choose-your-own-adventure, a 10-point list, and a one-idea-per-chapter book.

For my latest books, [10 Surprisingly Simple Ideas That Changed My Life]({% post_url 2026-03-04-10SimpleIdeas %}) and [Street-Smart Coding Manifesto]({% post_url 2026-07-28-StreetSmartCodingManifesto %}), I've skipped the prologue and the introduction.

From _Write Useful Books_, I learned to start non-fiction at Chapter 1.
It's like dropping viewers into the TV show drama instead of starting with a director's commentary.

[Start with blood]({% post_url 2025-05-09-BleedInTheFirstLine %}), drama, or an engaging story.

Don't bore readers or [waste their time]({% post_url 2026-05-05-WasteTime %}).
Give them something and give it fast.

_If you want to see this idea in action, you'll find [my books here](/books). Books on coding and personal growth that respect your time._
