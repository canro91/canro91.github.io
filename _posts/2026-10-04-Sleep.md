Poor sleeping habits can kill you. Literally.

Miss two days of good sleep in a row and you'll feel like a walking dead.\
And no amount of cups of coffee will help.\
I know because [I've struggled with my sleep]({% post_url 2026-09-13-Baseline %}) recently.

On a recent checkup, I brought up the subject with my doctor.\
Here's what she recommended:

**#1.** Have a light dinner and have it early, preferably while there's still sunlight.

**#2.** Before going to bed, turn off LED lights and go with a soft lamp in a corner.

**#3.** Keep electronic appliances out of your bedroom.\
No more TV, LED alarms, or phones.\
Sleep in a dark room.

**#4.** Go to bed early.\
Better to wake up early next day than staying in bed longer.

**#5.** Avoid drinking fluids after dinner and go to pee before going to be.\
And resist the temptation of get out of bed, if you're used to waking up to pee.

**#7.** As soon as you wake up, get some sun light to synchronize your circadian rhythm.

**#8.** Keep your stress under control.\
Stress raises cortisol and if your cortisol levels are already high, anything will wake you up at night.

[Sleep is your best productivity hack]({% post_url 2025-07-08-TheOnlyProductivityTip %}) and the easiest level to pull to [live healthier and longer]({% post_url 2026-03-11-Sleep %}).
