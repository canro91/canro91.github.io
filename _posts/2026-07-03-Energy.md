All productivity advice can be summarized into one single tip:

_Follow your energy levels._

Not in a woo-woo sense, but be aware of your energy levels.
1. Tackle your most important work when they're at its highest.
2. Learn to rise them: exercise, laugh, or walk.
3. [Keep your glucose spikes under control]({% post_url 2024-12-30-BestProductivityHack %}).

Notion dashboards, todo lists, and weekly goals aren't useful if you don't have any energy to tackle them.
