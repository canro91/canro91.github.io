A walk fixes almost anything.

To slow down my ruminating thoughts, I put on my green Nike shoes and sport clothes, and went to the closest park for [my daily walk]({% post_url 2026-07-01-Walk %}).

But to [avoid more overthinking]({% post_url 2024-12-19-TimeTravel %}), I added a twist.\
I choose to pay close attention to the sounds around me.\
The passing motorcycles, cars honking, kids screaming while playing soccer, some teenagers rehearsing a dance, a priest giving a sermon, or simply my footsteps on the pavement.

From time to time, I asked myself _"Are you breathing?"_\
Then I switched my attention to my breaths.

Without even realizing it, one hour has passed and [my mind already felt more quiet]({% post_url 2025-04-19-CalmDown %}).\
I guess I just discovered _Walking meditation,_ a new way to slow down and calm my mind.
