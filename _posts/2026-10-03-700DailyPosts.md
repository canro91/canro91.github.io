This is my daily post #701.

This last 100 posts felt like crossing the final line in a marathon, without realizing how you got there.\
It was just one foot in front of the other—or one post after the other.

My daily writing started in Nov 1st, 2024.\
I was recovering from burnout and writing was my way of [bringing joy back]({% post_url 2026-07-23-Recovery %}) to my life.\
I knew writing would take me somewhere else.\
And I was right.\
It's one of the habits has changed my life.

In these last 100 days, [my blog turned 8 years]({% post_url 2026-07-18-EightYearsOfBlogging %}), I finished another book, [Street-Smart Coding Manifesto]({% post_url 2026-07-28-StreetSmartCodingManifesto %}), and [I started a Substack publication]({% post_url 2026-09-26-Substack %}) to document my mission of [living free from burnout]({% post_url 2026-09-01-BurnoutFree %}).
