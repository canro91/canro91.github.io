**#1. If it drains you, cut it out.**\
Screens, social media, doomscrolling, sales dashboards, anything with a number next to your name...\
[Either it energizes you or it's out]({% post_url 2026-09-21-2026 %}).\
Time in front of a screen is time away from real interactions and yourself.

**#2. Stick to your baseline.**\
For over two days, [my overthinking didn't let me sleep]({% post_url 2026-09-13-Baseline %}).\
I woke up with my heart racing and breathing fast, just like when you had a bad dream with a love one dying in an accident.

I had to go back to exercising, sitting in silence, journaling, and [walking in nature]({% post_url 2026-09-22-CalmDown %}) to quiet my monkey brain.\
Have a set of daily healthy habits that keeps you regulated—and stick to it like your life depends on it.

**#3. If you work for it, you'll have a perfect life. But it won't be perfect all the time.**\
[Credits to my sister]({% post_url 2026-09-08-PerfectLife %}), when I opened up about my frustrations.\
Struggling is always part of the journey.

**#4. You get what you expect when you detach from the outcome.**\
The more you chase something, the more it gets further away.\
It's true for money, relationships, and pretty much anything else in life.

**#5. _"Better to have one handful with quietness than two handfuls with hard work and chasing the wind."_**\
From Ecclesiastes 4:6 (NLT).
Another proverb from one of the wisest man in history.

It came to my mind on a Monday evening when I was drinking a green smoothie and eating some olives at a cafe.\
Only tourists and retirees were around—and me.

Better to find contentment in [a boring but perfect Tuesday]({% post_url 2026-09-29-PerfectDay %}) than in a meaningless pursuit just for the sake of money.

**#6. There won't be a perfect occasion.**\
It's your job to [make every day a perfect one]({% post_url 2026-09-27-SelfCare %}).

**#7. Your frustrations, worries, and anxiety has something in common:**\
You and your mind.\
You create them and you have the power to control them.

_Keeping a healthy baseline is one of the [10 Surprisingly Simple Ideas That Changed My Life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/). Big plans and New Year's resolutions don't change your life. Small daily actions do._
