_Art is theft_, Picasso said.

And if art is theft, [Steal Like an Artist]({% post_url 2025-11-08-StealLikeAnArtist %}) teaches how to steal.\
I stole that book concept, a collection of 10 related but separate ideas, for _[10 Surprisingly Simple Ideas That Changed My Life And Could Change Yours Too](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/)_.
And that's only one of the [ideas I've stolen]({% post_url 2026-06-27-Stealing %}).

When starting out, it's tempting to chase originality.\
But it's easier to [fingerprint something]({% post_url 2024-11-21-PutYourFingerprint %}) or [make something old new again]({% post_url 2026-11-09-Creativity %}), just like love songs.
Originality doesn't come from isolation.

Once you're stealing, not only steal the art, [steal the process too]({% post_url 2026-06-07-Steal %}): how the art is packaged, launched, and sold.

Once you're copying from others, don't try to [copy from yourself]({% post_url 2026-10-08-Hits %}). That seldom works.
