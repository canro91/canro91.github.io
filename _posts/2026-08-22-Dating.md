This week, a friend shared how he learned what to look for in a relationship.

It was way back in college. He's now married with kids. One day, he saw an attractive girl on campus and stared. An older student noticed him staring and said: _"Hey man, I'm going to give you a piece of advice. Don't look for big boobs or butts. When all that falls down, the only thing left is talking."_

Many years later, he still remembers it. It reminded me of other [free pieces of advice I've received]({% post_url 2025-06-29-BestAdvice %}).
