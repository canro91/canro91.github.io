I found in Newsonat by Mark Rogers his [5 Ws of Reading](https://www.newsonaut.com/articles/my-five-ws-of-reading). So here are mine.

## WHO - An author you would love to have a ‘one to one’ with?

That's definitely James Altucher. I've binge-read almost all his books.

I found _Choose Yourself_ when I was on [my way out of burnout]({% post_url 2026-07-23-Recovery %}) and it inspired me to adopt a daily healthy practice—[that idea alone changed my life]({% post_url 2025-07-03-LifeChangingIdeas %}). Although [I've stalking James a lot]({% post_url 2024-11-11-LessonsFromJamesAltucher %}), that probably I'd have to come up with good questions.

## WHAT - What genre do you most gravitate to?

I try to balance between non-fiction and fiction. But these days, I'm more into non-fiction books, especially about physical and mental health, and well-being in general. In the last quarter, I've read a fortress about it: _[Emotional Intelligence]({% post_url 2026-09-20-EI %})_, _[Brain Wash]({% post_url 2026-10-06-BrainWash %})_, _Sleep_.

## WHERE - Where do you prefer to read?

As [a hack to read more books]({% post_url 2026-10-20-Books %}), I leave books on my kitchen table. So I found myself reading after breakfast as part of my morning routine.

## WHEN - What time of day do you prefer to read?

I balance between non-fiction in the morning, when my energy is at its peak, and fiction before going to bed to unplug.

## WHY - Why is your favourite book your favourite book?

I don't have A favorite books. To answer this, I'd have to do it per categories:

* For personal development, I'd go with _Choose Yourself,_ the raw failure stories and the idea of taking care as a first step to choose oneself.
* For finance, I'd go with _The Richest Man in Babylon._ It's the format itself: a fables instead of a boring list of facts and recommendations.

### A book that took me into a fascinating world

I read mostly non-fiction, not too many fascinating worlds, apart from our own worlds/heads. But I have on my reading list _The Lord of the Rings._

### A book that made me cry

It's not a popular one, but I co-wrote with my sister a book to honor our mom's memory: _Nuestros Recuerdos_ in Spanish. She wrote a good-bye letter to my mom I can't read. Every time I do it, I can't help by cry.
		
### A book that was thoughtful and profound

_Ecclesiastes_ from the Bible. OMG! It reads like the memories of an eccentric millionaire. Someone who wasted his entire life amassing wisdom and fortune, just to find out it was pointless. Of course, with lots of drops of wisdom on every page.

### A “comfort” book, read and re-read many times

Recently, I re-read _[Love in Times of Cholera]({% post_url 2026-08-26-Cholera %})_. I read it back in school, but I didn't enjoy it as much. Probably one of favorites by Gabriel García Márquez. I should have added it to my answer about favorite books.

## Bonus - How do you go about selecting what you will read next?

I don't have a clear strategy. I like to follow my curiosity or goals. These days, I'm interested in sleep, so that's what I'm reading about in next months... and I like to write in the blank page of a book a list of all books mention inside it... and Ryan Holiday's YouTube channel.
