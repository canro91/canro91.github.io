Unboxing the first copies of your book feels like opening Christmas presents.

It's the excitement of holding something that once existed only in your head and on post-it notes.\
It's the satisfaction of seeing an idea come to life after [an emotional roller coaster]({% post_url 2026-07-06-RollerCoaster %}).

In _[Story Telling: A Writing Life](https://www.isabelallende.com/en/book/storytelling/excerpt),_ Chilean author Isabel Allende writes,

> _"I once heard Elizabeth Gilbert say that we should not expect our writing to give us money or fame. We write because we love the process. I would add that we should not expect to publish either. Just do it."_

Money and fame can be rewarding.\
I don't want fame, but making a living from my words sounds appealing.

But the real reward is who you become by writing.\
It's seeing the world through a new pair of lenses.\
It's enjoying the quiet moments at the keyboard.\
It's dropping everything to write down an idea before you forget it.

[Writing keeps me healthy and alive]({% post_url 2026-04-19-WhyIWrite %}), and makes me insane.

After that first suggestion, Isabel Allende offers another piece of advice:

> _"Relax. Enjoy the process. Imagine that writing is like dancing, go with the flow, loosen up. It's not like lifting weights at the gym. The trick is less effort and more pleasure into it."_

Sometimes dancing is performing on a stage, in front of an audience.\
Other times, it's sharing an intimate moment with a loved one.\
And sometimes, it's putting on your headphones and moving your body when nobody is watching.

Just like dancing, it's the pleasure it brings that keeps you writing.
