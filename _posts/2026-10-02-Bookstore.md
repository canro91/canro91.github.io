_Do you have a book for parents raising two teenager girls?_, a guy said.

He was in the next aisle at a bookstore. I was looking for some Christmas presents. I had on my hands _Clear Thinking_ and _The Cat Who Saved Books_. I was looking for a third book to take advantage of the month's promotion. The cheapest of the three was free.

The guy kept walking the aisles. An employee started to recommend titles: _Think and Grow Rich_, _The 5:00 Club_... Then his daughter said, _"I've seen this book...I don't know the title, but its cover is a little brain made of 1-dollar bills..."_

_"The Psychology of Money,"_ I said laughing. That moment I knew she was my kind of reading mate. And I knew his after was doing something good: [surrounding them with books]({% post_url 2026-03-09-ReadingRules %}).

Then I saw it: sometimes a good book covers does all the heavy work for your book. Design your cover so a 13-year-old girl, probably not interested in your subject, could remember your book.

Like it or not, a book is judged by its cover. Just like [you and me are judged by the way we look]({% post_url 2026-04-22-Appearances %}).
