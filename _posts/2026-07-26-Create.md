Almost everybody In [my first AMA]({% post_url 2026-07-25-AMA %}) asked the same thing: _What helped you overcome burnout?_

The answer is simple: Bring back a sense of joy to your life.

Tim Denning wrote about it in [You're born to f*cking create](https://timdenning.substack.com/p/youre-born-to-fcking-create-life):

> _If you feel stuck, the easiest way I know how is to start writing publicly._
>
> _If you’ve got other creative goals, then do those. But make sure every day you are creating things and putting them out into the world to see what comes back._
>
> _Make creativity your #1 priority._

Burnout is disconnection.

To reconnect with yourself, go back to your hobbies or start new ones.
For me, that was writing a daily post.

Writing 200 words gave me a reason to wake up every day.
Writing about my career milestones felt like therapy.

Little by little, a hobby became serious.
One post led to [my first writing course]({% post_url 2024-12-26-WriteDaily %}).
Then it led to [a 100-post streak]({% post_url 2025-02-09-100DailyPosts %}).
Then to the idea of [writing a book]({% post_url 2025-04-22-BookForGrandkids %}).
Then to finally [calling myself a writer]({% post_url 2026-06-03-HoldATitle %}).
Then to [my horizon goal]({% post_url 2026-05-26-HorizonGoal %}): _documenting my life in books_.

Apart from [daily healthy habits]({% post_url 2025-07-03-LifeChangingIdeas %}), writing saved me—by giving me a purpose.
That's [why I keep doing it]({% post_url 2026-04-19-WhyIWrite %}).

If you're feeling disconnected or burned out, [diversify your joy]({% post_url 2025-01-07-DiversifyYourJoy %})—and go back to your hobbies. 

_Writing is one of the ideas in [10 simple ideas that changed my life]({% post_url 2026-03-04-10SimpleIdeas %})._
