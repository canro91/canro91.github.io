Being a coder, it's easy to get distracted building a complex pipeline to write a book.

Like [Chris who under-engineered his book](https://chriskiehl.com/article/how-i-under-engineered-my-book), I used a simple approach for [Street-Smart Coding Manifesto]({% post_url 2026-07-28-StreetSmartCodingManifesto %}):

1. Outline each chapter idea on post-it notes.
2. Write with Google Docs.
3. Share a copy with my beta-readers.
4. Import the master file on Reedsy.
5. Et voila!

Like [building a blogging engine]({% post_url 2024-09-23-StrugglingToWrite %}) instead of blogging, researching the best ax won't get a tree chopped.
