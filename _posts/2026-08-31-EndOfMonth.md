_As part of [my daily idea habit]({% post_url 2025-06-17-TenIdeas %}), yesterday I listed the actions I do at the end of every month. Here they are._

1. Make sure all bills are paid.
2. List 10 accomplishments from did in the month.
3. Backup my laptop.
4. Clean browser history on my phone.
5. [Track monthly income]({% post_url 2025-04-27-FinancesInOrder %}).
6. Create next month's expense note on my phone.
7. Sketch next month's habit tracker grid on my notebook.
