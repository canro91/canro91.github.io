One year ago, writing a book felt like a distant goal.

Today, _Street-Smart Coding_ turns 1 year old.\
It's the book I wish I had when starting out as a coder.\
A roadmap with 30 lessons to build skills that last a career.\
From the most conventional to the most unexpected.

I wrote it for my younger self Googling _"how to get better at coding."_

## _Street-Smart Coding_ became a book series

After one year, _Street-Smart Coding_ became the second book in a 4-part series.

I wrote a prequel, _[Street-Smart Coding Manifesto](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/streetsmartcodingmanifesto/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=one-candle-streetsmart-coding)_, and I'm working on a sequel, _[Street-Smart Coding in the Age of AI](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/streetsmartcodingintheageofai/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=one-candle-streetsmart-coding)_.\
I already have plans for a final career guide.

Those four will be my attempt to pass on everything I've learned about coding, and nobody taught me.

## What I would do differently

_Street-Smart Coding_ made me learn to write a book the hard way.

[I missed some steps]({% post_url 2025-11-25-MissedSteps %}) writing it.\
And, of course, today I'd do a couple of things differently:

1. I'd [write a shorter introduction]({% post_url 2026-07-29-BookChange %}).
2. I'd rewrite Chapter 1 to make it timeless. The principle is there: find your own answers. But I framed it using Google, which is not as relevant in these days of AI as it was years ago.
3. I'd [start writing it backwards]({% post_url 2026-10-27-Book %}).

[Writing a book taught me a whole lot]({% post_url 2025-11-16-LaunchingABook %}) about writing, publishing, and marketing.\
But more importantly, it gave me the confidence to [call myself a writer]({% post_url 2026-06-03-HoldATitle %}).

## Some random numbers

Unless you write a New York Times best-seller, you won't live off a single book.

But if someone outside your circle buys your books, it's already a success.\
_Street-Smart Coding_ is a huge success.

In the last year, it's sold 92 ebook copies via Gumroad, and 9 Kindle and 2 paperback copies via Amazon.\
It passed 100 copies in its first year.

Apart from sales, it earned me [my first hater]({% post_url 2025-11-02-Haters %}).\
Someone called me "b*tch" for promoting it.\
And it helped me define my category of one.\
Someone called me the "Street-Smart Coding guy" in a comment.\
Since then, I've made it my bio everywhere online.

Apart from my daily blog, _Street-Smart Coding_ is the project I'm the most proud of.

_One year later, I'd still recommend it to my younger self. If you want to code smarter and build real-world skills that last a career, grab your copy of [Street-Smart Coding](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/streetsmartcoding/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=one-candle-streetsmart-coding) today._
