_"I already know most of what's in there,"_ my sister told me.

We were in a bookstore downtown. She picked a book about reducing inflammation. That's something we both have been struggling with recently, for different reasons. When I asked if she wanted it, she said she'd rather skim.

If it teaches you at least one lesson, it's a good book. And if it doesn't teach you anything, it wasn't for you.

[Reading a book]({% post_url 2026-03-09-ReadingRules %}) is downloading decades of experiences and lessons into hours. It's the closest to the _"I know KungFu"_ experience of Neo in The Matrix. Reading three books on the same subject puts you ahead of 99% of people.

_I hope my books can give you at least 1 lesson worth keeping. [Find them here](/books)._
