_It's OK to re-read books._

If you only want to show off a book count, re-reading sounds like a waste of time.

But I usually find myself coming to a good book more than once:\
The first time because the subject was interesting.\
And the next time because I really needed the book.

This year, I've re-read _The Power of Now_, _[Choose Yourself Guide to Wealth]({% post_url 2025-01-19-ChooseYourselfGuideToWealth %})_, _[Brain Wash]({% post_url 2026-10-06-BrainWash %})_, and _Sleep_.

If someone recommends you a book you've already read, [go read it again]({% post_url 2026-09-30-Rereading %}).\
That's maybe when you really need it.
