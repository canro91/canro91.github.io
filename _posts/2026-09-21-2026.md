_If it drains you, cut it out._\
_If it energizes you, do it more._

One week free from social media taught me [I'm another junkie]({% post_url 2026-02-24-NoSocialMediaWeek %}).
My phone time showed me [I was an enemy of boredom]({% post_url 2026-02-03-Boredom %})—or still I am.
[Analog Sundays]({% post_url 2026-05-18-AnalogSundays %}) helped me to reconnect.
But for some reason, or for some distractions, I stopped following them.
And I regretted it.

A new social platform, my own expectations, and rushing to chase an imaginary finish line took a toll on my physical and mental health.
They cost me two nights without proper sleep.
And they force me [back to my daily practice]({% post_url 2026-09-13-Baseline %}).

Less scrolling.\
[Less phone time]({% post_url 2026-01-22-LessScrolling %}).\
Less rushing.\
Less consuming.

More self-care.\
More time in nature.\
[More being present]({% post_url 2026-01-11-BecomeWiser %}).\
More creating.

Bring a sense of joy and fulfillment to your life every day. [Make every day a good day]({% post_url 2026-09-03-GoodDay %}).
