_I recently watched Codie Sanchez's video on 10 upgrades to make once you have more money. It reminded me of [a 10-idea list]({% post_url 2025-06-17-TenIdeas %}) I wrote. Here it is._

1. Healthcare. No more ER waiting lines.
2. Shoes, mattress, and chair. No more back pain.
3. Diet and food. No more cheap fast foods.
4. Library. No more ebooks or pirated books.
5. Dedicated reading room. No more reading on the couch.
6. Air conditioning in common areas. No more heatwaves in summer.
7. Workspace. No more virtual backgrounds hiding mess in meetings.
8. Home workout equipment. No more waiting for machines at a gym.
9. Hire a virtual assistant. No more unpleasant tasks.
10. Accountant and financial advisor. No more taxes.
