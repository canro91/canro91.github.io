For my latest experiment, I'm writing 30 notes and 4 posts on Substack.

Here's how my 2nd week looked like in some numbers:
* 8 followers, still 0 subscribers
* 6 notes, 1 post
* 6 likes on the top performing note
* 5 likes and 1 comment on the next top performing one
* 4 Note formats stolen (as an artist)

A well-known creator liked almost all my notes, after I replied to one of hers. I guess that bumped it up a bit.

If you're curious, here are [my first impressions after one week]({% post_url 2026-08-29-Substack1 %}).

## A mission and next steps

The most important bit of my experiment so far, [being a multi-passionate]({% post_url 2024-12-16-FindYourPassion %}), I found my next mission:

> _Create a healthy and purposeful lifestyle free from burnout, CVs, and interviews._

That goal covers exercising, nutrition, writing, money creation, and digital minimalist. And that's what I'm interested in these days.

For the next week, I'm sticking to sharing 1 notes, leaving 5 comments, and liking 10 notes. Or whatever happens before 15 minutes. That's [how I use social media consciously as a writer]({% post_url 2026-01-19-LinkedIn %}).
