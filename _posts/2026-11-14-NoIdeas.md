Daily blogging teaches a skill as important as writing: _finding ideas_.

Since November 2024, I've been writing daily.\
That's when I decided to [write seriously]({% post_url 2025-07-01-SomethingSeriously %}), to recover from burnout.\
For months, I was afraid of [running out of ideas]({% post_url 2025-05-20-200DailyPosts %}) and [I've almost quit]({% post_url 2026-03-16-500DailyPosts %}) more than once.

But as Nikhil wrote in [The Mind Doesn't Run Out](https://nikhil.bearblog.dev/the-mind-doesnt-run-out/):

> _Running out of ideas is a myth. Your mind is far more expansive than you imagine. Some days I feel I could write to the ends of the earth and still not want to stop. Other days, even a single paragraph is a struggle._

For over 700 days, I've kept my daily streak, with more effort some days than others.\
But a commitment to publish pushes me find anything to share:\
A quote, a mundane story, a reaction to a post, a lesson, a coding trick. Anything.

When you look at the world through some writer's eyes, you find material or inspiration anywhere.\
That's [the main effect of writing]({% post_url 2026-04-08-WriteDaily %}) and enough proof that running out of ideas is a myth.

Writing has turned me into an idea hunter... and [that habit has changed my life](https://imcsarag.gumroad.com/l/10simpleideas/).
